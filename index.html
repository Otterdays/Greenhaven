What you can do

Small verbs. A real world.

You wake in Deepwood, walk out along one faint track, and Ottertown is already busy being a place — with other players in it, or without.

Talk to the meadow

Enter opens chat and reaches everyone in your world. Villagers talk back, and some keep shops — you start with 100 coins and a merchant who deals in sand and clay.

Fern: anyone by the quarry?
Bartholomew Goldthread: Everything is for sale. Even this conversation.
Moss: just hit 30 mining, gold rocks open
You caught a minnow. +20 Fishing.
Server-authoritative

The world decides

You send where you want to go; the world server decides where you stand. No slipping through a tree on the diagonal, no rubber-banding into the river.

Characters that stay

Name, place, skills, coins and inventory are saved with every visit. One account, three worlds. The meadow does not forget you.

Chop, mine, dig, fish

Nine trees, twenty rocks, six fish, and sand and clay in the ground. Every harvest is checked by the world and every point of XP is kept.

Sprint the trails

Hold Shift to run at double speed. Fresh ground under your feet trains Stride — loops around one tile pay nothing. The dust follows your boots.

Look closer

F5 stands you inside your character's eyes, M unrolls the full world map, P opens your skills sheet. F1 lists every key.

Runs on your machine

A native window, not a browser tab. Rust world server, Bevy client, PostgreSQL saves. Windows and Linux today; no account needed to wander Single player.

Skill ladders

Train while you wander.

Four skills climb to 99 and every point of XP is saved. Pick a habit and the meadow pays you back. The numbers below are the real ones from the shared rules crate.

Level 1 – 99

Woodcutting

Nine tree kinds, roughly one every ten levels — from the young oak by your first campfire to the elderwood at 80.

Do I need an account?
Not to wander. Single player walks the map with no server and nothing on the wire. To keep a character across visits and worlds, create an account from the log-in screen inside the game.
Is it really free?
Yes. There is no shop, no payment, no premium anything. Coins in the game are for the merchant.
What is "Early Development"?
One developer, one meadow, four skills, no combat yet. Things change between versions and occasionally break. The world server is authoritative, so nothing you do can corrupt someone else's character.
Where is my character saved?
On the world server, in PostgreSQL, keyed to your account. Positions flush every five seconds and when you leave; XP and inventory are written when they change.
