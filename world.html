Overworld

The world

A 250×250 map you can walk end to end: a decked village square at the centre, a ring road, a winding river with three timber bridges — and Deepwood, the clearing where you wake up.

The ground carries you

  • Meadow, trail and road
  • Boardwalk, dock and corduroy
  • Timber bridges over the river

These stop you

  • Water and fishing spots
  • Forest and old-growth trees
  • Stone, rocks and clay mounds

Who is in charge

You send where you want to go. The world decides where you stand, so you cannot slip through a tree on the diagonal.

Landmarks

What is out there.

Colours change, the path bends, and something worth clicking waits at almost every turn.

Across the map

  • A decked village square with four gate roads and a ring road around it.
  • A winding river crossed by three timber bridges.
  • A south-west lake with two piers and a promenade.
  • An orchard in the north-west, and a farmstead with a herd pen.

Worth the walk

  • A stone circle standing quiet in the fields.
  • A south-east quarry stacked with ore and gem veins.
  • A north-east birch grove around a small pond.
  • Deepwood: old-growth, a cold campfire, four young oaks, and one faint track out.

Six seats by the fire

Saved for later: Nick the master mechanic and gamer, Kate the methodical organizer, Lytle the determined protector, Bianca the mother goose, Remington the aspiring young mind, and Nate the house painter (Killa). They are loved in the developer's life; someday, echoes of them may walk the meadow.

Play now

Walk it yourself.

Download the native client and the first thing you will see is Deepwood. Everything after that is up to your feet.
