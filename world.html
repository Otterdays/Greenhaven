OVERWORLD

The world

The meadow of Greenhaven: a wall of trees, a river, a timber crossing. Walk the grass and trails. The water and the woods stop you.

Ground

  • Meadow, trail, timber — walkable.
  • Water, forest, stone — they stop you. You begin on ground you can walk.

Who is in charge

You send where you want to go. The world decides where you stand, so you cannot slip through a tree on the diagonal.

Futures

Six seats by the fire are saved: Nick the master mechanic and gamer, Kate the methodical organizer, Lytle the determined protector, Bianca the mother goose, Remington the aspiring young mind, and Nate the house painter (Killa). They are loved in the developer's life; someday, echoes of them may walk the meadow.
