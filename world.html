Overworld · Ottertown

Two hundred and fifty
tiles each way.

A 250×250 map you can walk end to end: a decked village square at the centre, a ring road, a winding river with three timber bridges — and Deepwood, the hidden clearing where you wake up.

Decked square Ring road & gates River, lake, pond Timber bridges Old-growth forest Quarry Landmark
01

Deepwood

Where you wake: old-growth, a cold campfire, four young oaks, one faint track out. The first 25 XP in the game is right here.

02

Village square

Decked boards, four gate roads, a ring road around it, the merchant's stall and the sign that welcomes you to Ottertown.

03

The river

Winds from the north edge to the south, crossed by three timber bridges. Fishing spots along the banks.

04

The lake

South-west, with two piers and a promenade. The quietest place to fish and the longest walk from the square.

05

The quarry

South-east, stacked with ore and gem veins — copper at the mouth, diamond at the back for those with the level to open it.

06

Orchard & farmstead

North-west. Rows of chop-ready trees, a farmhouse and a herd pen where the cows drift after each other.

07

Birch grove

North-east, around a small pond. White birch is scenery — it shades the path and refuses the axe.

08

Stone circle

Standing quiet in the southern fields. Nobody has explained it yet. The Historian may.

The rules of the ground

What carries you. What stops you.

The ground carries you

  • Meadow, trail and road
  • Boardwalk, dock and corduroy
  • Timber bridges over the river
  • Ramps up onto the quarry shelves

These stop you

  • Water, reeds, lilies and fishing spots
  • Forest and old-growth trees
  • Stone, rocks and clay mounds
  • Fences, walls and stalls

Who is in charge

You send where you want to go. The world decides where you stand, twenty times a second, so you cannot slip through a tree on the diagonal — and neither can anyone else.

Who lives here

Twenty souls and a herd.

B

Bartholomew Goldthread

Merchant · the square

Plum coat, plumed hat, coin purse always full. Buys and sells sand and clay. Never breaks character.

R

Ryan the Historian

Chronicler

Grey beard, teal robe, leather tome. He remembers the town before it was Ottertown, back when the old name hung over the gate.

J

Eighteen jesters

Wanderers

Bells and motley, drifting over walkable ground. Inspect one; each has a line.

C

The herd

Livestock · farmstead

White cows with brown patches. They graze where they stand, drift after each other, and shy away when you crowd them.
