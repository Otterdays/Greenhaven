Getting started

How to play

Greenhaven is a native window, not this site. Download the client, pick a name, and you are standing in the meadow.

Download for Windows Download for Linux
Three steps

From download to Deepwood.

  1. 1

    Open the client

    A short illustrated opening plays first — click anywhere, or press Space or Esc to skip it. You land on the main menu.

  2. 2

    Name your character

    1–20 letters, numbers, spaces, - or _. Whatever you pick floats above your head in the world.

  3. 3

    Play, or wander Single player

    Play joins the world server when it is running. Single player walks the demo map with no server at all. Quit or Esc leaves.

Inspect, chop, mine, dig Click
In the meadow

What happens when you click.

  • Click a young oak to chop — your axe swings, leaves shake, and XP floats up over your head.
  • Click rocks to mine. Copper opens at level 1 and diamond at 90, and the rocks never run out.
  • Click sand or clay to dig; the materials land in your inventory sheet.
  • Click a fishing spot, the jester, a cow, or a placed villager to inspect. Named villagers can talk back, and some keep a shop — you start with 100 coins.
  • Enter opens chat and reaches everyone in the world. Names float above bodies as you pass.
  • Hold Shift to sprint the trails. Fresh ground trains Stride; the dust follows your boots.
Why you cannot walk through a tree: online play is checked by the world server, so the world always knows where you really stand. Single player runs the same shared rules locally.
Ready

Pick a name. Wake up in Deepwood.

Your character is saved with every visit — leave today, come back next week, and the meadow remembers.
