GETTING STARTED

How to play

Greenhaven is a native window, not this site. The site is the front porch. Open the client to walk the meadow.

Download Windows client

Main menu

  1. Download the Windows client, then open it. It lands on a menu. Nothing talks to the world until you click Play.
  2. Set your character name (1–20 letters, numbers, spaces, - or _).
  3. Play or Enter connects. Quit or Esc leaves.

In the meadow

  • WASD or arrow keys move. The world decides where you actually stand.
  • Enter opens chat. Esc closes chat, or returns to the menu if you are not typing.
  • Play again later and your character is still there.
