v0.1.1428 Sep 2026Release · Halloween

Hallowmere opens — and fighting got real

A walled Halloween town now sits north of where you spawn: graveyard, haunted high street, giant-pumpkin plaza, hay maze and a festival green, all lit by ghost-green lanterns. Seven new creatures stalk it — from a shy black cat to Jack Hollowgourd, the Pumpkin King (lv 45) — and hostile mobs now attack you: server-dealt damage, a health bar that counts down, respawn at the village with nothing lost.

v0.1.1428 Sep 2026Combat

Pets you earn in the wild

The black cat and vampire bat are now fightable mobs — and each has a low chance of dropping its matching familiar when you slay it. A pet earned this way is yours for good; summon it from the Pets tab. Monsters with a loot table drop their items on death now, too.

v0.1.1428 Sep 2026Feel

A closer camera and a livelier body

The view starts nearer your character and now zooms to 600% (was 400%). Your character breathes, shifts its weight while idle, sprints in a smooth 4-frame run, and winds up and follows through on every attack — 21 poses per direction.

v0.1.1325 Sep 2026Release · The big one

Forty times the world, and your first punch

The biggest update Greenhaven has had. The starting world grew to 256×192 — roads, two wooden bridges, fishing ponds, four tree species and meadows in bloom, all loopable on foot. You can punch things: click a monster and combat skills (Attack, Strength, Defence, Archery, Wizardry, Health) work the way the old-school greats do. Coins got a bag with five tiers, every chest became your bank, and merchants can stock any item in the game.

v0.1.1325 Sep 2026World

Meet Rudolph

A gift-only reindeer pet that follows you everywhere, fetches coins off the ground, joins your fights, carries four slots of your bag — and levels up doing all of it. Other players see him trotting along beside you.

v0.1.1325 Sep 2026Comfort

Chat that waits, music that stays

Chat now stays on screen while you type and messages fade one at a time. Background music plays through the whole world, online and in Single player — including Moon Over Bamboo, a new original lullaby. And the yellow harvest boxes are gone: a pointing hand shows what you can click.

v0.1.1325 Sep 2026Economy

Shops, rebuilt

The shop panel is two sides — buy left, sell right — with tooltips for every item. Merchants stock anything, up to 40 items each, and limited stock stays sold after a restart. A rare item is finally, properly rare.

v0.1.1325 Sep 2026Seasons

The Christmas wardrobe completed

Santa hat, festive jumper, trousers and elf boots — plus the Nutcracker hammer, which earns 10% extra smithing XP. New Skilling slot frees a bag space for your tools, and bronze axes and pickaxes became smithable.

v0.1.1224 Sep 2026Seasons

String lights and a dozen new props

RGB bulbs now run between any two lamps — placed in the painter, moving with the lamps, rainbow-cycling in game. Twelve new festive pieces joined the palette: a decorated tree, snowman, sleigh, gingerbread house and more.

v0.1.1224 Sep 2026World

The meadow got untidy (on purpose)

Organic dirt paths with worn edges, ground patches, per-cell variation on every tree and rock, new chest finishes, five new environments — and a shaded, rimmed minimap that finally reads at a glance.

v0.1.1122 Sep 2026Feel

Your character, sharper and livelier

Player art redone at three times the detail, a real two-step run animation, blinking during idle breath — and Left Ctrl to crouch into a quiet kneel-walk. The premium shop got an evergreen-and-gold storefront, and Willowmere Lullaby joined the playlist.

v0.1.10September 2026Release

One PLAY button, three worlds, a hello

The main menu was rebuilt around a single PLAY button and a world chooser that finds Worlds 2 and 3 on its own. A short note from the dev greets you once per launch. "Remember username" on the log-in screen; passwords are deliberately never saved.

v0.1.10September 2026Feel

Chop, dig and level-up effects

Bark chips fly off the tree, soil puffs off the spade, and a level-up shines the badge and sweeps the XP bar once. Everything respects the reduced-motion setting.

v0.1.8September 2026Release

Fishing joins the ladders

Six tiers of fish on one shared harvest contract. Nodes deplete and respawn on the server, so the pond you fish is the pond everyone fishes.

v0.1.8September 2026World

The town gets its name

Ottertown, on the gate sign and on the map panel. The Historian remembers what it was called before.
